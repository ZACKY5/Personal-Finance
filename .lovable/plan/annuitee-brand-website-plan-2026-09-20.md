# Annuitee Brand Website Plan

## Goal
Create the first complete visual and narrative expression of **ANNUITEE**: a premium consumer financial brand built around calm, decisive financial thinking. The site will establish brand, story, trust, and product promise without pretending the full advisory product already exists.

## Visual System
- Build the identity from the supplied palette: Ink Black `#0D0E17`, Space Indigo `#252943`, French Blue `#474C80`, Porcelain `#FFFEF6`, Beige `#F8F7E2`, Periwinkle `#B7B9D7`, and sparing Burnished Copper `#C9825B`.
- Use **Manrope** for navigation, controls, labels, body copy, and product language; use **Newsreader** selectively for emotional headlines and manifesto moments.
- Create a restrained typographic ANNUITEE wordmark with a subtle custom letter treatment, plus a compact mobile treatment.
- Use editorial spacing, asymmetrical compositions, fine rules, near-square corners, restrained shadows, and large type rather than conventional fintech cards.
- Develop a recurring “complexity into clarity” motif: fragmented fine lines and financial notations resolving into one copper path. Use custom brand artwork rather than stock finance imagery.
- Design light and dark themes independently, with a visible theme control and accessible contrast in both.

## Shared Experience
- Create a refined sticky navigation with Home, What We Do, Vision, People, Tools, and an always-accessible **Build My Plan** action.
- Build shared wordmark, navigation, mobile menu, theme control, buttons, editorial section primitives, product preview, line motif, disclosure treatment, and minimal footer.
- Add restrained scroll reveals, typography transitions, tactile link/button states, and reduced-motion support.
- Keep the Build My Plan action connected to the illustrative planning experience, without presenting it as a live recommendation engine.

## Pages

### Home
Build the most developed page as a continuous editorial story:
1. Human-problem opening with “Knowing what to do with your money shouldn’t be this hard,” concise supporting copy, and two actions.
2. “Most people don’t have a money problem. They have a decision problem.” expressed as a typographic narrative, not a feature grid.
3. “Less noise. More direction.” showing personal inputs resolving into **Your Financial Plan**.
4. A polished conceptual monthly-plan preview using the supplied illustrative ₹35,000 allocation and an explicit placeholder-value disclosure.
5. The decisive philosophy: Understand → Think → **Decide** → Act.
6. Four typographic steps for how it works.
7. A quiet subscription cue: financial plans should change as life changes, without prices.
8. Editorial vision statement and a dramatic minimal final CTA.

### What We Do
- Explain “Financial thinking, made personal.”
- Present the sequence from life and financial picture through goals, risk, strategy, and action.
- Focus on decision quality and personal context rather than features or technical claims.

### Vision
- Create a spacious manifesto led by “Everyone should have access to good financial thinking.”
- Explore personalization, clarity, access, and the limits of more information.
- End with the brand line.

### People
- Keep the page intentionally sparse.
- Show four clearly labeled role placeholders: Founder, Product, Investment / Finance, and Technology.
- Use abstract editorial portrait areas without invented names, credentials, or claims.

### Tools
- Introduce the future ecosystem with NPV Calculator, SIP Calculator, Goal Planner, Retirement Planner, Portfolio Check, and Risk Profiler.
- Mark every item **Coming Soon** and avoid fake functionality or dashboard styling.

## Trust and Content
- Keep copy short, human, direct, and free from hype or jargon.
- Add subtle prototype and future regulatory/compliance disclosures where relevant.
- Do not add testimonials, customer logos, awards, usage figures, returns, performance claims, or fabricated people.
- Make clear that the plan preview is illustrative and not financial advice.

## Responsive and Quality Checks
- Preserve the editorial hierarchy and whitespace on mobile rather than compressing desktop layouts.
- Verify navigation, theme switching, CTA links, mobile menu, typography, and all five pages at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Check for overflow, overlap, readability, accessible focus states, reduced motion, and deliberate dark-mode surfaces.
- Give each page unique title, description, Open Graph metadata, and social card metadata.
- Run the project’s automated validation and inspect the finished pages in the browser before completion.

## Technical Scope
- Use the existing React/TanStack site structure and shared route layout.
- Add separate routes for Home, What We Do, Vision, People, and Tools.
- Keep all visual values in the semantic design system and reusable components.
- No database, authentication, subscription billing, calculators, onboarding, or real investment logic in this phase.
